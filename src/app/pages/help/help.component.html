Start here

Your workspace connects properties, open houses, visitor submissions, and leads. A property can have several open houses; each event has its own visitor page, QR code, question set, and feedback.

  1. Review My Profile and complete your editable contact details and default questions. Agency-owned agents use agency-managed branding and brokerage information; ask your agency administrator to update those locked details.
  2. Add a listing in Properties, or create the property while setting up an event.
  3. Create an Open House, choose questions, and mark the ones needed on paper as printable.
  4. Review the event preview, test the visitor link and QR code, and download your printed materials.
  5. After visitors submit, review their answers and follow up from Leads.

This page is a read-only reference. Opening links takes you to the relevant workspace; reading this guide does not change your records.

Profile & branding

Open My Profile to review your name, email, phone, real estate license number, and headline. Use the section’s edit button to update details. Check the information visitors will use to contact you before sharing an event.

The Brokerage section shows your brokerage information. For independently managed accounts with editing access, use the section’s edit button to update it. Where branding editing is available, you can upload a headshot and logo and choose primary, secondary, and accent colors. Image uploads accept JPEG, PNG, and WebP. Choose clear images and colors with readable contrast.

Use Preview Public Branding to check the visitor-facing appearance. Review the actual event visitor page too before printing or distributing its QR code.

Agency-owned agents: locked branding & brokerage

Your agency manages your branding and brokerage information. These sections are read-only for agency-owned agents, so editing controls may be disabled or unavailable. This is expected and is not a loading or sign-in problem. Contact your agency administrator to request corrections to the agency-managed branding or brokerage details.

You can still review the displayed information and preview your public branding. Use the editing controls available for your own profile and default questions. Check the public event page after your agency updates its details, and download fresh printed materials when needed.

Default feedback questions

Your saved defaults provide a starting question set for new events. Review both Required and Printable settings. An event can use your defaults or its own selection. Saving a custom selection as defaults makes that selection available for future events; check existing events separately when you need to change their questions.

Properties

Use Add Property to enter the listing address and price. Include the unit or second address line when needed. Expand a property row to review its address, listing price, status, open-house history, and event feedback. Use the property actions menu to edit details or archive or restore the listing.

Use the Active and Archived views to find listings. Archiving moves a property out of the active view; it is not a delete action. Select multiple property checkboxes to use the bulk archive or restore action. Clear the selection when you are finished.

For a repeat event at the same address, select the existing property instead of creating another copy. Each open house remains a separate event, so you can review feedback by date.

In the expanded row, View event feedback reveals the selected event’s submissions. Click Hide event feedback to collapse them, or select another event’s feedback button to switch events.

Creating & editing open houses

  1. Open Open Houses and choose the create action.
  2. Select an existing property, or choose Create Property and complete its details.
  3. Set the start date, start time, event duration, and end time. Check the schedule carefully, especially for a two-day event.
  4. Use your default feedback questions, or turn that option off to customize the selection.
  5. Select questions and mark at least one selected question as Printable.
  6. Click Preview and review the event details and materials.

Preview saves the event before opening its preview. If you need to change a saved event, expand its row and choose Edit Open House from the actions menu. Preview Changes saves your updates and reopens the preview.

If no questions are printable, a dialog stops the save and asks you to go back and select questions. Choose Go back and select questions to reveal the custom question selector. Your entered details stay in the form.

Expand a saved event to review its property, public code, schedule, question counts, visitor link, QR code, and print previews. View questions and View feedback reveal their lists; use the corresponding Hide button to collapse them.

Choosing questions for digital & paper forms

Selected
Includes the question in the event’s feedback question set.
Required
Marks a selected question as required for the digital feedback form.
Printable
Includes the selected question on the paper feedback form.
Digital Only
The selected question is available digitally but is left off the paper form.

Select a question before marking it Required or Printable. Deselecting a question also clears its Required and Printable settings. Question types include single choice, ratings, short text, and long text.

The paper form has a limited layout capacity. Longer answers and questions with more choices use more space, so the capacity is not a simple question count. If another question cannot be marked printable, remove another printable question or use a shorter question.

If you enable Save these as my default questions, the custom selection is saved as your profile defaults when the workflow succeeds. Leave it off when the selection is only for this event.

Visitor links & QR codes

Expand an open house and find Visitor Page. The public URL opens the visitor-facing page for that event. Share that event’s link with visitors; the agent portal address is for your own workspace.

The QR code opens the event’s public page. Scan it with a phone before putting materials on display. Verify the property, agent contact information, and questions. Each event has its own public code, so check that a repeat event uses the intended event’s materials.

If you change event details, reopen the visitor page to verify the result. Download updated printed materials after changes to the schedule, branding, or printable questions.

Printed materials

In an expanded open house, open Open House QR Sign or Printable Feedback Form under Printable Materials. Each preview can be expanded or collapsed independently. Use its download button to obtain the material.

The previews target US Letter paper (8.5 × 11 inches). Review your printer’s paper size, scaling, and margins. Check that the QR code and all answers remain readable, and test a printed QR code before visitors arrive.

The QR sign directs visitors to the digital page. The paper form contains only questions marked Printable. Writing on a paper form does not automatically create a digital submission or lead. You can use Add Lead to record contact details and notes manually; this does not recreate the full feedback submission.

Viewing visitor submissions

Visitor feedback is available from three places:

  • Open Houses: expand the event and choose View feedback to see submissions for that event.
  • Properties: expand the listing and choose View event feedback beside the relevant date.
  • Leads: expand a lead to see the submissions associated with that person.

Submission lists show the visitor, submission date, and event context when available, with the newest submissions first. Choose View feedback beside a submission to open the answer dialog. Review the visitor contact details, linked property and event, and recorded answers. Close the dialog to return to the list.

Multiple visits can produce multiple submissions. Use the event and date to distinguish them. An anonymous label or missing contact field means that information is unavailable on that record; it does not mean the feedback itself is missing. Optional questions may have no answer.

A “No feedback submissions” message means there are no submissions in that context. On a property, check another event if the visitor attended a different date.

Leads, follow-up & notes

Open Leads to review your contacts. Expand a row to see contact details, buyer profile information when available, submissions, and notes. Email and phone links open the corresponding application on your device.

Use Add Lead for a contact collected outside the digital visitor form. The expanded row’s actions menu lets you edit contact details or mark a lead lost. Editing contact information does not rewrite the visitor’s original feedback answers.

To update several leads, select their checkboxes, choose a status in the bulk actions bar, and click Update Status. Choosing the status alone does not apply it. Check the selected count first, then clear the selection when finished.

Use the Active and Closed lead views to find contacts. The Closed view shows leads with Closed status. If a lead seems to disappear after a status change, check the other view. Add notes to record conversations, preferences, and the next follow-up step. A manually added lead may have no buyer-profile answers or submissions yet.

When something does not work

Preview does not advance

Check required fields and validation messages in both the property and event sections. Confirm that a property is selected, the schedule is complete, and at least one selected question is printable. If you are using defaults with zero printable questions, follow the dialog back to the selector and enable Printable on a selected question.

A question is missing from the paper form

Check that it is both selected and Printable. Required alone does not add a question to paper. If Printable cannot be enabled, the form may be at capacity. Save changes and download a fresh copy.

Feedback is not visible

Click the event or property feedback button to reveal submissions. Verify the event date and property, especially when an address has multiple open houses. Confirm the visitor completed the digital submission; opening the page or filling out paper does not create a digital record. Refresh the page after a new submission if needed.

A row says it could not load details

Close and reopen the expanded row to retry. Check your connection. If the problem continues, refresh the page and sign in again if prompted. Save or copy unsaved form entries before refreshing.

A property or lead is missing

Check the Active or Archived property views, or the Active or Closed lead views, as appropriate. Confirm you signed in to the intended account. Reopen or refresh the list after a change if the displayed data appears stale.

The QR code or visitor link does not open

Open the public URL directly to distinguish a link problem from a scanning problem. Check your connection, the full URL, and that the code belongs to the intended event. Use a clear, unobstructed print with enough contrast and space around the QR code.

A download or contact link does nothing

Check your browser’s download panel and any blocked-download notice. Email and telephone links require a configured application on your device. You can copy the displayed contact information instead.

I cannot edit branding or brokerage information

Branding and brokerage information are locked for agency-owned agents. Ask your agency administrator to make the change. Refreshing the page or signing in again will not unlock agency-managed settings.

Branding looks outdated

Review the displayed branding, reload the public branding preview, and open the actual event page again. If you have editing access, verify your saved changes. If your account is agency-owned, ask your agency administrator to verify the agency-managed details. Previously downloaded documents do not update themselves; download a new copy after changes are saved.

Access is pending or sign-in fails

Use the account associated with your agent profile. If the app displays pending access, wait for the account owner or platform administrator to confirm activation. Refreshing cannot grant access. For sign-in issues, use the options shown on the sign-in page and check that the correct email was entered.

The problem continues

Record the page, action, event date or property, exact error text, and approximate time. A screenshot can help explain the problem when you contact your existing support contact. Avoid including passwords or unnecessary visitor contact information.
